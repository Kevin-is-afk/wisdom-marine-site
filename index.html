Nine service lines under one roof

Docking and afloat repairs

Dry docking in Dubai Maritime City, barge and supply boat construction, and afloat repairs across the U.A.E. Work is planned to minimize vessel downtime.

  • Dry docking of ships and barges in DMC
  • Construction of barges and supply boats
  • Afloat repairs at all U.A.E. ports (except Fujairah)

Steel, piping and hydraulics

Steel renewals, pipe lines, high pressure hydraulic lines and structural works, fabricated and fitted to the job.

  • Steel and piping renewal
  • Hydraulic tube renewal

Mechanical and machine shop

All mechanical and hydraulic repairs, on site or in our workshop, whichever the job needs.

  • Mechanical repairs and overhauls
  • Precision machine shop services

Surface treatment

Hydro and grit blasting followed by painting works, from steel plate to finished hull.

  • Hydro / grit blasting
  • Airless spray painting

Work from the yard

Policies

The written commitments that guide how we work with crews, owners and the environment.

Safety policy

Wisdom Marine is committed to providing a framework for setting occupational health, safety and environmental performance objectives.

  • To achieve continuous improvement in the HSE management system through education, training, consultation, reward and recognition programs, auditing, constant monitoring, evaluation and review.
  • Ensure the health and safety of workforce, the environment, related parties and public by adhering to the local, national and international legal and other requirements.
Customer service policy
  • Provide progress updates at agreed intervals.
  • Maintain open communication for technical queries and change requests.
  • Commit to warranty repairs for workmanship defects within agreed period.
Quality policy
  • Provide our customers with the best value for our products and services.
  • Strive for continual improvement by setting quality objectives and targets.
  • Lead the employees to work as a team and create an environment to obtain better business results.
  • Comply with applicable statutory and regulatory requirements related to products and services.
  • Continually comply with the requirements of ISO 9001-2008 and improve the effectiveness of our quality management system.
